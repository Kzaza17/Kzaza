# Afrin Times: Listen

A listening app for the premiere issue of **Afrin Times** (Autumn 2026, Issue 01), a digital quarterly on Kurdish history, culture and identity.

It pairs the 224-page magazine with a 42-minute audio deep dive. As the presenters talk, the page they're discussing appears on screen, the transcript highlights word by word, and the matching article text follows along.

**▶ Open it: https://kzaza17.github.io/Kzaza/**

## Features

- **Synced pages:** 60 magazine pages appear on screen at the moment they're discussed
- **20 chapters:** jump straight to any topic, from the 1841 poem "Revendus Bey" to Sykes–Picot, Şerif Pasha, Layla Bedir Khan and the Kurdish horse
- **Reading mode:** tap any paragraph to play the part of the audio that covers it
- **Transcript mode:** word-by-word captions; tap any line to jump there
- **Full-screen player:** chapter markers, 15-second skips, speeds from 0.75× to 2×
- **Remembers where you were:** your position and saved chapters are kept in your browser
- **Works on phones:** Android and iOS, no app or install needed

## How it works

Everything is in a single `index.html` file: the audio, page images and transcript are built in, so it runs without a server or internet connection. The audio was transcribed and each topic was matched by hand to the page being discussed.

## Using it offline

Download `index.html` and open it in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). On iPhone, open it from the Files app's share menu in a browser, as the built-in preview won't play audio.
